// 05 — Transparency Report
Known Issues
3 Active
01
Cracked Java Access
High
Cannot be fixed due to host-side Proxy restrictions on the free managed plan. The login authentication bypass requires direct access to Proxy config files which MineKeep does not expose on the free tier.
Workaround
Use Bedrock Edition on mobile, Windows 10/11, or via an Android emulator.
02
Low RAM Performance
Medium
Occasional stutters and TPS drops because we're on a free hosting tier with limited RAM allocation. Heavy activities like TNT explosions or large mob farms may cause noticeable lag spikes.
Workaround
Migrate to a paid plan. Free tier limitations cannot be optimized further at the plugin level.
03
Bedrock Player List Visibility
Low
Bedrock players connected via Geyser may not appear in the website's player list even when online. This is a limitation of the server's query protocol — Geyser-bridged players aren't always exposed through the standard player list API. Their count is included in the total, but names and skins can't be retrieved.
Status
Investigating query protocol config options. No workaround available on the free hosting tier.
